After that I went into drug discovery. At Genentech's Prescient Design I used machine learning to design antibodies. Then I co-founded Coefficient Bio, where we spent our time figuring out which molecules were worth making at all. Now I'm at Anthropic, working on AI that can plan experiments and learn from how they turn out.

When I'm not working I'm usually playing volleyball, climbing, surfing or snowboarding. This magazine is where I put the thoughts that don't fit anywhere else.
